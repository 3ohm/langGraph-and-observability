# P17: Bounded evidence (caps and the code budget)

## Problem

Evidence grows with the incident: a crash-looping pod has megabytes of logs and hundreds of events. Unbounded evidence bloats every checkpoint, slows each step, and can exceed the model's context or the run's cost.

## What the template does

- Every evidence item goes through `make_item`, which redacts it and then caps its text at `MAX_EVIDENCE_TEXT` (4000 characters). Redaction runs first, so a cut never splits a secret.
- A log keeps its end, where the crash is; other text keeps its start. A cut is marked `TRUNCATED`.
- Logs: the last `APP_LOG_TAIL_LINES` (500) lines, of the current and the previous container. Events: at most 50. Datadog: at most `APP_DD_MAX_EVENTS` (50) events, in a window of 30 minutes before to 5 minutes after the incident.
- Code: snippets are ranked (the top app frames first) and kept while their text fits `APP_CODE_BUDGET_CHARS` (60,000).
- The schemas cap the analysis too: summary length, cited evidence, suspected code and actions.
